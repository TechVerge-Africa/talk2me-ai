# Talk2Me AI Product Principles

Talk2Me AI is built on the belief that communication is a human right. These principles guide every product decision we make.

## 1. Invisible Tech, Human First
Technology should disappear. The AI interpretation, captioning, and media transport should feel so seamless that users focus on each other, not the platform.
- **Goal**: Sub-100ms latency for sign-to-speech.
- **Metric**: "Zero-thought" communication.

## 2. Participation Parity
Inclusion isn't just about adding captions; it's about ensuring a Deaf participant or a non-native speaker can lead a meeting as effectively as anyone else.
- **Goal**: Full feature parity across all accessibility modes.
- **Constraint**: If a feature isn't accessible, it isn't ready for production.

## 3. Premium Aesthetics for All
Accessibility features (like high-contrast modes or large captions) should never feel like "assistive tools" that look secondary. They should be integrated into our premium, "Talk2Me" design language.
- **Design System**: Glassmorphism, smooth animations, and high-fidelity typography.

## 4. Privacy as a Pillar
Conversations are sacred. We do not use participant media to train general AI models without explicit, transparent opt-in.
- **Security**: Strict RLS policies and ephemeral room state.

## 5. Performance is Accessibility
Low-end devices and poor network conditions are accessibility challenges. We optimize for high performance to remain inclusive for users in all regions, including TechVerge Africa's primary markets.
- **Metric**: 90+ Lighthouse score on Mobile.

---

## The Inclusive Standard
- **Speech-to-Text**: For the hard of hearing.
- **Sign-to-Speech**: For the ASL/BSL/International Sign community.
- **Text-to-Speech**: For non-vocal participants.
- **Real-time Summaries**: For cognitive accessibility and focus.
