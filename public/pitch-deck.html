01. The Problem

The 40%+ Accent Gap & The Meeting Void

Over 1.4 billion people across Africa navigate workplaces where global communication tools systematically break down.

🚨

Catastrophic Accent Failure

OpenAI Whisper, Zoom, and Google Meet hit 40%+ Word Error Rates (WER) on Ghanaian Pidgin, Twi, and African-accented English. Meeting transcripts garble names, currency, and strategic decisions.

📢

Acoustic & Bandwidth Hostility

Outdoor markets, street traffic, and diesel generators overpower traditional noise gates. Fluctuating 3G/4G connections drop heavy video feeds and cause desynchronized audio.

🕳️

The Post-Call Black Hole

When video calls end, commitments vanish into thin air. Teams manually juggle fragmented notes across Slack, WhatsApp, and Jira with zero verifiable attribution to spoken audio.

⚠️ Global BPOs and remote tech teams hiring African talent lose thousands of billable hours monthly re-explaining verbal client instructions.
